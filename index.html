How alerts work

The app checks the latest NEA readings every few minutes while it is open, and you get one notification when the level reaches your threshold. You are alerted again only after it has dropped back below.

With no server involved, alerts while the app is closed depend on your browser: Chrome on Android can check in the background for an installed app (not on a fixed schedule). iPhones and iPads cannot check in the background.
